HOW TO DETERMINE STOP:
Stop should be the minimium size that gives us
1-WR > 50%, psychological / operational / drawdown
2-Edge > 50%, Profitability of system
3-Adjacent areas have acceptable performance
4-Can handle the worst market condition
5-Performs good enough in good market conditions

It seems that stop 15% and target 30% is the sweet spot
Almost 2 x ATR (30) @ M1 for QQQ, out of noise but tight enough 
to exit the trade relatively soon in market crashes

TRUE RANGE:
MED TR @ M1 = 8.3%
MED TR @ M3 = 16.3%
MED TR @ M12 = 35%

OPTIMAL EXIT:
Stop = 2 x MED TR @ M1
RRR = 2

How can I connect this to swing trading?

Let's review swing trading performance on RRR=1. for 2000-2010 period:
M1: 10,10: 0.49, 75%
M3: 15,15: 0.64, 83%
M12: 35,35: 0.92, 100%

The important finding is:
1-Increasing the stop and target (RRR=1) increases both WR and Edge.
2-Increasing the stop at a fixed target increases WR but decreases the edge.
3-Increasing the target at a fixed stop increases the edge but decreases WR. 

But what is the optimal stop for swing trading?
In swing trading we're going to use the smallest stop that satisfies edge and psychology
critera.

M1 (with RRR=1) / stop = 10% seems to be smallest stop that satisfies both. This means M1 swing trading works.
Of course we're going to acheive higher RRRs but as a baseline RRR=1 works in bull markets.
